Hermes NJ Vault Assistant

Hermes NJ Vault Assistant is a private, family-use assistant run by Nihas Babu. It helps one household organise its own notes, reminders and documents.

It connects only to the family's own Google account to:

  • save and find the family's documents in their own Google Drive,
  • read and send the family's own email in Gmail,
  • manage the family's own calendar events, contacts, Docs and Sheets.

It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and does not sell or share any data.
