SVG Drive Sync

SVG Drive Sync is a private, internal tool of Smart Value General Contracting LLC (UAE). It keeps the company's own project files in its own Google Drive account (smartvgm@gmail.com) available on the company's office computer.

It is used only by the company's own staff. It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and does not sell or share any data.
