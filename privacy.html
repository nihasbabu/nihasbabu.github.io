Privacy Policy – Hermes Vault Assistant

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Hermes Vault Assistant ("the app") is a private assistant used only by the family of Nihas Babu. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Who can use it

The app is used only by the account owner's own household. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google account: Gmail (read, send, organise), Google Drive files, Google Calendar, Google Contacts (read only), Google Docs and Google Sheets.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the family asks for, such as saving a document to Drive, finding a file, reading or sending an email, or adding a calendar event. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Notes created from this data are stored on the family's own devices. Google data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. The access token is stored only on the family's own computer.

Limited use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
